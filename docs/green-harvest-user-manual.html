Workspace

Overview and finance

Overview summarises the company’s recorded activity. Finance is the main place to review money movements and monthly totals.

Overview

Use the Overview cards to scan recorded cash position, income, expenses, and active projects. Below them, review investment phase totals, account summaries, and recent transactions. These figures reflect entries in the system; they are only as complete as the records entered.

Record a transaction

1
Open Finance and select Record transaction under Transactions.
2
Choose whether money came in or went out, then enter the date, a clear description, currency, and amount.
3
Choose the account. Optionally link a project, investment phase, and receipt or payment reference.
4
For AED, choose a saved AED-to-UGX exchange rate. Review the details and save the transaction.
Reporting currency: company totals are shown in UGX. AED transactions retain the exchange rate used when the record was saved, so historical values stay tied to that rate.

Field operations

Farm locations and crop cycles

Set up each farm or field as a location, then record the crops grown there as crop cycles.

Add farm locations first

Go to Company settings → Farm locations → Add. Enter a recognisable location name and, if useful, a short size note. These locations can then be selected for crops, stock, projects, and assets.

Record a crop cycle

1
Open Farm operations and select Record crop cycle.
2
Enter the crop or block name (for example tomatoes, soya beans, or cabbages) and its variety if known.
3
Select a farm location and status: Planned, Growing, Harvested, or Closed. Add area and start date.
4
Record total crop cost, harvest quantity, and sales revenue when known. Save the crop cycle.

Read the crop cards

Each card shows the location, area, start date, crop status, recorded total cost, sales revenue, profit, and return on investment when cost is non-zero. Link sales to the matching crop cycle so its sales revenue and profitability can be reflected accurately.

Keep crop names consistent. Use a clear name and variety for each cycle. Create separate cycles for different fields, planting periods, or varieties when you need to compare them.

Crop cycles are recorded from this screen. Farm locations are maintained in Company settings.

Supply and inventory

Procurement, suppliers, and stock

Register suppliers and inventory items before recording purchases and stock movements.

Set up suppliers and items

Supplier

Select Supplier. Enter the supplier/business name. Contact person, phone, email, and address are optional but helpful.

Stock item

Select Stock item. Enter the item name, category, unit, opening quantity, reorder level, and store/farm location.

Stock categories include seeds, fertiliser, chemicals, tools, packaging, produce, and other items. Use a consistent unit such as kg, bags, litres, or pieces.

Record a purchase

1
Select Record purchase; enter the date, category, item or service name, and UGX cost.
2
Optionally enter purchased quantity, supplier, the stock item being replenished, and the project the purchase supports.
3
Add expected benefit or receipt/proof reference if useful, then save.

A purchase is recorded as an expense. When linked to a stock item with a quantity, it also adds the received quantity to stock.

Issue or correct stock

In Stock on hand, select Adjust next to an item. Choose Issue / use stock to record stock used, or Add stock correction to increase the recorded balance. Enter quantity, date, and reason. The system prevents issuing more than the available quantity.

Reorder alert: the stock page highlights items whose on-hand quantity is at or below their reorder level. Review this list when planning orders.
Customers and revenue

Produce sales and customers

Record each sale so the sales register and company financial records stay aligned.

Register a customer

On Produce sales, choose Add customer. The customer/business name is required; contact person, phone, email, and address can be added for future reference.

Record a sale

1
Select Record sale, then enter the sale date and produce name.
2
Enter quantity, unit, and unit price in UGX. The app calculates the sale total.
3
Select a registered customer, or leave it as a walk-in customer. Optionally link the crop cycle.
4
If the produce is in stock, select the produce stock item to reduce its balance. Select payment method and add notes if needed, then save.

Available payment methods include cash, mobile money, bank transfer, credit, and other. Saved sales appear in the sales register and contribute to recorded income. Crop-linked sales can also be reflected on the crop card.

Before saving: verify quantity, unit price, customer, crop cycle, and whether stock should be reduced. If the produce is not tracked as a stock item, leave the stock selector unchanged.
Company resources

Staff, attendance, payroll, and assets

The People & assets page brings together staff and equipment records with daily attendance and payroll activity.

Add or update a staff member

Choose Add person and enter the name. Job title, department, email, phone, start date, status, monthly salary in UGX, and notes help maintain a useful staff register. Use the pencil icon on a person’s record to update details.

Record attendance

  1. Choose Record attendance.
  2. Select an active employee, date, and status: Present, Absent, Leave, or Half day.
  3. Add an optional note and save. The latest attendance records appear on the page.

Record payroll and payments

Choose Record payroll, select an employee, and enter the pay-period start/end dates, gross salary, and amount paid now. The employee’s monthly salary can prefill gross salary. If a payment is made now, record its payment date.

For a later or partial payment, use Pay on the payroll entry. Enter the amount and payment date; the page shows the outstanding balance. Payroll and salary payments are recorded as expenses.

Record company assets

Choose Add asset and record its name, category, asset tag/serial, acquisition date, cost, location, assigned person, status, and notes. Status can be In use, Maintenance, or Retired. Use the pencil icon to update an asset record.

Payroll tip: check the pay period and gross salary before saving. Record later payments against the same payroll item to keep paid and outstanding amounts clear.
Planning and oversight

Projects, decisions, settings, and audit

Track a project budget

In Projects, choose Create project. Enter a name, department or location if relevant, planned budget, forecast revenue, status, main risk, next milestone/date, and optional project picture. The project card compares planned budget with actual spending recorded against that project and shows the amount remaining or overspent.

To make the comparison useful, select the correct project when recording an expense or purchase. Project status can be Planned, Active, or Completed. Pictures are optional.

Log an investment decision

Open Investment decisions and select Log decision. Record the date, decision/investment name, amount in UGX, reason or expected return, and approver. This provides a register of the choice and its rationale.

Company settings

Use Company settings to review the organisation profile and reporting defaults, add Cash/Bank/Mobile money accounts, create departments, and maintain farm locations. Add these before entering operational records that need them.

Audit log

The Audit log is a review screen for recorded activity history. Use it when you need to check who performed a recorded action and when it was logged.

Quick reference

Good records and getting help

Good recording habits

  • Enter records promptly and use the date the event actually happened.
  • Use clear descriptions and consistent crop, stock, and unit names.
  • Link expenses and purchases to the relevant project to improve budget comparisons.
  • Use the correct supplier/customer, farm location, account, and crop cycle when available.
  • Check the amount, currency, quantity, and date before saving. After saving, confirm the item appears in its register.
  • Keep supporting receipts and payment references available to authorised company staff.
